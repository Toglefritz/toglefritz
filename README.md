My work spans **API design, distributed cloud services, mobile applications, and internal tooling**, with a strong emphasis on **developer experience, correctness, and long-term maintainability**.

In practice, this means working fluidly across the stack, from **front-end product development** to **shared infrastructure and tooling**, and turning complex, ambiguous problem spaces into **clear abstractions, well-considered tradeoffs, and scalable systems** that are both powerful to build *and* intuitive to use.
